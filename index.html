    Why I made this

    I have egg and wheat allergies. In everyday life I can read labels and ask questions. The hard moments are the ones where I can't: abroad, in a hurry, or in a disaster shelter where volunteers change every shift. A card that says the same thing every time, in the reader's own language, removes the guesswork for both sides.
