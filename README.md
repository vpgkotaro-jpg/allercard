# AllerCard

Make a clear food allergy card in Japanese, English, Chinese or Korean, show it full screen on your phone, or print it for your wallet. Plus a short emergency-kit checklist for people with food allergies.

**Live site:** https://vpgkotaro-jpg.github.io/allercard/

## Why
I have egg and wheat allergies. The hard moments are the ones where I can't explain myself: abroad, in a hurry, or in a disaster shelter where volunteers change every shift. A card that says the same thing every time, in the reader's language, removes the guesswork for both sides.

## Features
- 13 common allergens (Japan's labelling list plus tree nuts, fish, shellfish) and a free-text field
- Two levels: "severe, even a trace" and "please tell me"
- Card in 4 languages, with a small English line underneath so anyone can double-check
- Full-screen mode for showing a phone across a counter; print / save as PDF
- Emergency kit checklist
- Keyboard and screen-reader friendly, dark mode, works offline once loaded
- No account, no server, no tracking. Everything is saved only in your browser (localStorage)

## Tech
Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step, no libraries. Hosted on GitHub Pages.
